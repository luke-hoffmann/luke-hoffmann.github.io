### Random Thoughts

I appreciate the freedom to do what I want when I want. I think it's cool how there are tons of students, indoor and outdoor, who are just sitting down at a laptop, doing work. Cool how between classes I have breaks where I can do what I look. Hang out with friends, do what I need to do. Do homework. The post office is really easy. I get an email when I have a package or mail arrived and I can go and pick it up. I have spoken with a lot of people, and my friends continually have said how outgoing and sociable I am. I knew I was that, but it's interesting to have people notice that from the outside. It's just who I am. I really have enjoyed talking to random people.

Researching which clubs to join, and putting a lot of intentional effort into where I will be investing my time. Lots of options in college, but important to be very choosy with what receives my time and effort.

Each day that passes is another day that either something happens, or doesn't happen.

Everyone seems much more kind and sociable than at Lynden. Easier to have conversations with people. People are open to talking and just messing around. At Lynden, it was so cliquey.
