It was good to take time off from Cal Poly. I got some good clarity, and it was nice to have time to think.  

Sometimes life can be go go go, from one thing to the next. Going from highschool to college immediately, just because “thats what people do.”  

Im glad I took time off because I was able to reflect on why i was moving to the next step of college. It’s now my decision instead of just “blindly” following the typical route.  

Learning from this experience, I know that I should take some deciding, and weighing my next steps forward in life. Part of The Toyota Way says to “Make decision slowly by consensus, thoroughly considering all options; implement decisions rapidly.” I think this is a good principle for decision making in my life. Going to college in the Fall, I spent some time considering the options (work, trade school, college, etc.), but I think spending more time would have helped me gain clarity on why college was the right choice.  

So moving forward, I will try to more deliberately consider different options for any big decision in my life.
